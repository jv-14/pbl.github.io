AIM To understand the working of Optical fibres,explore their applications in the modern world and to check the necessary conditions for the optic fibre to work.

WORKING Fiber optics transmit data as light through thin sheets of glass or plastic. Each fiber consists of a core, where the light travels through it, and a surrounding cladding that reflects the light back into the core part. Data is converted into light using a laser or LED, and these light pulses travel through the core, bouncing off the cladding to stay contained in it. Because the light must strike the boundary with an angle greater than the critical angle, only light that enters the fiber within a certain range of angles can travel down the fiber without leaking out. This range of angles is called the acceptance cone of the fiber. The size of this acceptance cone is a function of the refractive index difference between the fiber's core and cladding.
